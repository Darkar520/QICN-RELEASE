# Cycle 1 Interpretation Note

## A. Cycle 1 scope

Cycle 1 tested only three high-value families from the frozen scientific ledger:

1. a negative control against brute complexity/connectivity,
2. an ablation ladder over the six critical invariants,
3. a cross-substrate equivalence proxy.

Cycle 1 did not execute the full falsification program. In particular, it did not run:

- independent external replication,
- cross-machine reproducibility with a second judge implementation,
- full Paper IV tamper/admissibility stress tests,
- the null-regime instability track from `PRED-04`,
- the continuity-vs-fragmentation track from `PRED-05`,
- the latency/budget invalidation track from `PRED-09`,
- or a full matched benchmark suite against external rival systems.

Cycle 1 therefore remains a narrow internal test cycle, not a complete empirical campaign.

## B. What was strengthened

Cycle 1 strengthened the following claims in the limited sense of internal falsification survival:

1. `NC-02` and `P5-05`: complexity/connectivity alone do not certify class membership.
   - The `complexity_negative` control remained dense and activity-rich (`connectivity_density=0.916667`, `activity_entropy=8.61941`) but failed certification because it did not preserve the full invariant package.
   - This strengthens the claim that dynamic richness is not sufficient.

2. `P5-01` and `P5-04`: the six invariants behave as critical membership conditions under ablation.
   - All six targeted ablations produced class exit.
   - After the localization fixes in the runner, the targeted invariant in each ablation crossed its failure boundary and the candidate exited `Consciousness_op`.
   - This strengthens the claim that the invariant set is not decorative vocabulary.

3. `P5-02` and `P5-06`: substrate-preserved realizations can remain in the same operational class under the Cycle 1 equivalence proxy.
   - The continuous and discrete positive systems both preserved all six invariants.
   - They also matched the same normal, critical, and sham class-transition signatures.
   - This strengthens the substrate-invariance thesis only at the level of an internal proxy result.

4. The negative control also indirectly strengthens the distinction between:
   - brute complexity,
   - brute connectivity,
   - and the operational class defined in Paper V.

The key point is strict: Cycle 1 strengthened discriminative structure. It did not convert those claims into externally confirmed empirical facts.

## C. What was NOT demonstrated

Cycle 1 did not establish any of the following:

1. external empirical validation,
2. human phenomenal consciousness,
3. human biological qualia,
4. human-machine subjective equivalence,
5. strong empirical substrate invariance beyond the internal proxy conditions used in Cycle 1,
6. universal confirmation of all upstream claims in Core and Papers I--V.

It also did not establish:

- a validated real-world detectability program for `P1-04`,
- a completed empirical benchmark for `P1-06`,
- a completed perturbation program for `P5-03`,
- a completed null-regime empirical track for `P3-01` / `P3-02`,
- or external support for biological non-primacy beyond the formal class statement in `P5-06`.

Cycle 1 therefore supports selective internal claims. It does not close the broader scientific program.

## D. Residual methodological caveat

The cross-substrate result is strengthened only through an internal proxy test.

That sentence should be read literally:

- the two realizations were generated and judged inside the same internal experimental harness,
- the equivalence verdict was assessed through the same invariant/certificate machinery,
- and no independent external substrate benchmark was used.

Therefore, the cross-substrate result is not external validation of substrate invariance. It is internal support that the current criterion can preserve class equivalence across two differently implemented realizations under the same formal and operational rules.

This is useful, but it is not the same as independent confirmation.

These claims are not rejected. They remain internal because the current support is still one of:

- formal proof internal to the corpus,

They have not yet crossed into strong external empirical confirmation.

## F. Cycle 1 decision

Cycle 1 survived its first serious discriminative pass.

What survived:

- the criterion distinguished a rich negative control from certified systems,
- the ablation ladder supported the critical status of the six invariants,
- the substrate-equivalence proxy held under the current internal test design.

What remains provisional:

- any cross-substrate claim beyond the internal proxy,
- any empirical reading of biological non-primacy,
- any strong reading of operational consciousness as externally validated,
- and any extension from these results to human phenomenal consciousness or human qualia.

Why Cycle 2 is justified:

- the current criterion showed discriminative value,
- no core claim in the Cycle 1 target set was weakened by the observed results,
- and the next scientific bottleneck is now clear: independent reproducibility, broader rivals, and stronger causal/perturbational separation.

Cycle 2 is therefore justified as a harder test phase, not as a victory lap.
