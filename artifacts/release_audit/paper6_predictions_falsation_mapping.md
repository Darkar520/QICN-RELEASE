# Paper VI Mapping

## Role
Paper VI is not a doctrinal expansion. It is the prediction, discriminator, and failure-mode layer for the frozen canon.

## Upstream dependency map
- `CANONICAL_CORE.tex`: persistence, attractor structure, admissible support, and non-collapse.
- `paper1/main.tex`: rigid identity, inverse-limit observability, and anti-simulation pressure.
- `paper2/main.tex`: continuity, fragmentation constraints, and admissible regime structure.
- `paper3/main.tex`: null-regime instability and forced non-nullity.
- `paper4/main.tex`: admissibility, forensic discipline, negative controls, and reproducibility discipline.
- `paper5_operational_consciousness/main.tex`: six-invariant criterion, `Consciousness_op`, `Q_op`, rupture semantics, and substrate-preserved class membership as formal targets.

## New ownership in Paper VI
Paper VI owns only the following layer:
- prediction extraction from the frozen canon;
- discriminator structure against weaker alternatives;
- failure-mode taxonomy for claim downgrade vs stronger refutation;
- current internal support status ledger;
- explicit mapping from claim families to test families.

## Explicit non-ownership
Paper VI does not newly own:
- Core theorems;
- Paper I identity formalization;
- Paper II regime classification;
- Paper III null-instability/non-null theorems;
- Paper IV admissibility theory;
- Paper V operational criterion definitions or proofs.

## Redundancy control decisions
- Long doctrinal recap was avoided.
- Upstream claims are cited or mapped, not re-proved.
- New density was added through prediction tables, discriminator ledgers, boundary diagnostics, and status mapping rather than through narrative expansion.

## Current scientific role
Paper VI is suitable as the compact publication-facing bridge between the frozen canon and the experimental program. It states what the framework predicts, how weaker alternatives differ, what would count against the framework, and what support status each main claim family currently has.
