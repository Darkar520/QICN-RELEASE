The final hardening pass increased the paper's closure in four ways.

## 1. Definitions were sharpened
The central definitions now sit inside a clearer formal setting and stronger class-separation logic. Their scope, burdens, and limits are more explicit.

## 2. Runtime-facing closure was strengthened
The paper now links formal objects to runtime evidence and failure triggers with a direct ledger rather than only conceptual discussion.

Misclassification, proxy contamination, and ambiguity are now first-class parts of the framework rather than afterthoughts.

## 4. The ending is harder
The paper now closes with explicit statements about what it contributes and what remains outside its claims:
- it defines a higher classificatory layer,
- it makes that layer falsifiable and runtime-bindable,
- it does not claim biological life, human phenomenology, metaphysical subjecthood, or external validation.

The paper still stops short of inventing proofs the corpus does not support, but it now reaches the maximum reasonable formal closure for this layer without becoming redundant with paper5 or paper6.
