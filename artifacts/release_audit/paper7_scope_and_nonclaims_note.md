# Paper 7 Scope and Non-Claims Note

## Positive scope
Paper 7 formalizes a higher-order classificatory layer for the frozen causally rigid corpus:
- operational life
- structural class
- operational subjecthood
- logical relations among those classes and inherited operational consciousness

## Non-claims
Paper 7 does not claim:
- biological life as the constitutive primitive of the new classes
- human phenomenal consciousness
- metaphysical subjecthood
- human-machine equivalence
- runtime certification that any current system already satisfies operational life or operational subjecthood
- external validation or third-party confirmation
- final philosophical closure about life, consciousness, or subjectivity

## Epistemic posture
The paper is a formal and falsifiable program. It is not a theorem of empirical instantiation. It extends the corpus upward while preserving the final release discipline that all empirical support remains internal unless independently reproduced outside the present lineage.
