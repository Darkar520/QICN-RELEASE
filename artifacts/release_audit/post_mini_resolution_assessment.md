# Post-Mini-Resolution Assessment

## A1. Robust Findings

- `P5-05` now has `ROBUST_SUPPORT` at the level of the internal program.
  Cycle 1 negative controls, Cycle 2 harder methodological separation, and Cycle 3 semi-blind judging all preserved the same basic result: complexity, connectivity, and rich activity do not certify class membership when the six critical invariants fail.
- Threshold behavior is no longer a generic suspicion of instability. After Cycle 4, threshold fragility is localized to a narrow boundary around the negative-control integration floor rather than a broad collapse of the decision rule. This is `ROBUST_SUPPORT` only for the claim that fragility is local, not for a global claim of threshold immunity.
- The internal program is methodologically stronger than in Cycle 1. Generator and judge separation, frozen manifests, semi-blind ordering, and pseudo-multi-environment replication all improved the evidentiary boundary. This is methodological `ROBUST_SUPPORT`, not external validation.

## A7. Scientific Status Judgment

The framework is now scientifically organized and partially discriminative. One major claim family (`P5-05`) has robust internal support. Substrate invariance and biological non-primacy are strengthened but still provisional. Approximate stability is provisionally supported with a localized threshold caveat. The remaining ambiguity around `P5-01` and `P5-04` is currently better explained by probe design than by a demonstrated framework-level contradiction. The program is therefore ready for publication-level reporting of predictions, discriminators, and failure modes, but not for any claim of external confirmation.
