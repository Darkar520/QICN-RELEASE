\begin{abstract}
This document presents the foundational mathematical layer of the Rigid Identity Framework. It consolidates the core theorem-level apparatus on which downstream papers depend, without borrowing hypotheses from later application layers. Specifically, this base monograph proves contractive projection dynamics, typed computable witnesses, parameterwise non-collapse under explicit anti-constant assumptions, inverse-limit identity under non-finite-determination assumptions, conditional rigidity under admissible perturbation models, conditional obstruction to uniform response fidelity under a binary representation budget, and operational-criterion grammar together with explicit falsation and scope boundaries. It does not establish phenomenality, metaphysical subjectivity, human equivalence, runtime instantiation, cross-mode bridge admissibility, or external adjudication.
\end{abstract}
