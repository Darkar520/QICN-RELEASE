\section{Methodological Scope Clarifications}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item Under strict state contraction ($\norm{K}<1$), state persistence with unit eigenvalue $\lambda_1=1$ is excluded; stability accounting relies instead on positive spectral gaps.
\item Explicit anti-collapse witnesses require typed embeddings rather than untyped symbolic representatives.
\item Non-collapse assumptions are formulated parameterwise to preclude constant fixed-point collapse across $\U$.
\item Inverse-limit non-locality is explicitly conditioned on the non-finite-determination hypothesis (NFD), rather than asserted unconditionally over arbitrary set-theoretic encodings.
\item Rigidity and non-simulability theorems carry explicit metric, perturbation, budget, and observation-family assumption blocks.
\item Substrate invariance, biological comparisons, and cross-mode bridge admissibility remain outside the scope of BaseCore.
\end{enumerate}
