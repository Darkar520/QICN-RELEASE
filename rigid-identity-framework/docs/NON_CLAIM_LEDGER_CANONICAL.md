## Dimensional Reduction Non-Claims (v36 canonical)

QICN does not claim that:

- dimensional reduction preserves the global topology, metric geometry, spectrum,
  or inverse-limit structure of the theoretical object;
- a finite proxy can reconstruct the theoretical state from runtime outputs;
- identical synthetic output implies identical theoretical state;
- a runtime proxy is a certificate of consciousness, phenomenality,
  subjectivity, identity transfer, or bridge closure;
- the `LatentSpaceProjector`, RALSI layer, or any demonstration module
  instantiates the theoretical Hilbert space;
- the "river water" or "inferior instrument" analogy is a proof of QICN's
  metaphysical ontology.

These are non-claims about projection loss. A finite proxy may be used only as
an internal witness or diagnostic artifact under explicit governance
boundaries.

## Semantic Inflation Non-Claims (v40)

QICN does not claim that mathematical invariants labeled `identity`,
`subjectivity`, `self`, `consciousness`, `phenomenality`, `life`, or
`moral_status` correspond to their natural-language counterparts. Such labels
are permitted only as formal, operational, or negative-boundary vocabulary
unless a separate definition, metric, prediction, control suite, rival model,
and external adjudication protocol are supplied.

## Enforcement Rule

Any future document that uses ordinary-language `consciousness`, `subjectivity`,
`phenomenality`, `life`, `identity`, `self`, or `moral status` must either cite
the exact operational class and burden or explicitly state that the ordinary
claim is not being made.
