## Anti-Inflation Clause

No result from this template may be described as external validation unless it
was executed by an independent adjudicator or replicated under the Paper 10
external-adjudication architecture. No result may be described as proof of
consciousness, phenomenality, human equivalence, identity transfer, moral
status, or metaphysical subjecthood.
