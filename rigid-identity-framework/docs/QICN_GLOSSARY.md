# QICN Glossary

Status: canonical terminology scaffold for v36/v37 governance.  
Boundary: this glossary defines terms for internal formal and runtime
governance. It does not certify consciousness, phenomenality, identity
transfer, external validation, or bridge-burden closure.

| Term | Definition | Layer | Non-claim boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| finite proxy | A finite runtime object that approximates or witnesses a selected formal property without preserving the full theoretical object. | R | Not a certificate of consciousness or theoretical equivalence. |
| information deficit under Pi | The unrecoverable loss induced by a non-injective projection `Pi` from a richer theoretical domain to a finite runtime representation. | T/R bridge | Does not quantify phenomenality unless a separate operational metric is defined. |
| non-injective projection | A map `Pi:X -> Y` for which distinct states in `X` can share the same image in `Y`. | T/R bridge | Equal runtime outputs do not imply equal theoretical states. |
| fiber | The preimage `Pi^{-1}(y)` of an observation value `y`; the set of theoretical states indistinguishable by the finite observation. | T | A large fiber is projection loss, not evidence for or against consciousness. |
| kernel | For a linear map `P:H -> V`, the subspace `{h in H : P(h)=0}`. Use only in linear settings. | T | Do not use `kernel` for arbitrary category-theoretic maps. |
| inferior instrument lemma | A formal statement that a finite or lower-capacity observation instrument cannot reconstruct every distinction in a richer theoretical domain. | T | Pedagogical support for projection loss, not proof of QICN ontology. |
| river water analogy | Informal illustration of the inferior instrument lemma. | M/T illustration | Analogy only; not a formal argument for consciousness or ontology. |
| DEMONSTRATION_MODULE | A runtime or visualization module that illustrates a mathematical limitation without participating in adjudication as evidence. | R | Must not be promoted to external support or certification. |
| lower-bound support | A restricted internal diagnostic showing that a proxy satisfies declared finite conditions. | R | Does not imply bridge closure or external validation. |
| non-certifying ladder | A chain of proxies, gates, and reports that can block overclaims but cannot certify phenomenality or identity transfer. | R governance | A passed local ladder remains internal unless external protocol conditions are met. |
| null regime `emptyset_phi` | Bottom element of the phenomenological regime poset when defined inside `E`. | T | Distinct from undefined assignment. |
| undefined assignment `bot` | Marker outside `E` indicating absence of an admissible assignment. | T/R typing | Must not be conflated with `emptyset_phi`. |

## Semantic Inflation Notes (v40)

The v40 semantic-inflation audit marks the following terms as high-risk because
their measured operational declaration ratio satisfies `I(T) < 0.5`. These
notes do not redefine ordinary language; they constrain QICN usage.

| Term | v40 inflation note |
|---|---|
| identity | Use `identity` as a mathematical or operational label only when the specific invariant, object, metric, or claim burden is named. It does not imply personal identity, metaphysical identity, or identity transfer. |
| consciousness | Use only as an explicitly bounded target term, negative boundary, or future adjudication object. It does not imply human or phenomenal consciousness. |
| phenomenal | Treat `phenomenal`, `phenomenality`, and `phenomenology` as ordinary-language risk terms unless an explicit formal regime, observable, or non-claim boundary is cited. |
| subjective | Treat `subjective` as a high-risk ordinary-language adjective unless attached to a defined operational coordinate or explicitly denied as an ordinary claim. |
| self | Treat `self`, `selfhood`, and self-like labels as bookkeeping or formal labels unless a separate operational burden is stated. They do not imply metaphysical selfhood. |
| moral_status | Treat moral status language as prohibited for promotion unless an external ethics protocol and independent review record are supplied. QICN currently makes no moral-status claim. |
