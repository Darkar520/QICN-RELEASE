## Update Rule

Any claim may move upward only with a linked preregistration, artifact set,
negative-control result, rival comparison, and decision record. Any failed
destruction condition must trigger downgrade or withdrawal.

## Downgrade Hooks

| Trigger | Required status action |
|---|---|
| `PRED-04c` decision is `destruction` | Mark pass-region stability and dependent null-regime claims as `refuted` or `high_risk_control_surface` until a versioned reformulation exists. |
| `PRED-06` accepts any tampered run | Mark Paper 4 admissibility as `high_risk_control_surface`. |
| `RIVAL-COMPLEXITY-01` matches QICN with lower penalized loss | Downgrade irreducibility-dependent `Cop` and `I_int` readings to `internal-support` at most. |
| Any registered negative control passes certification | Freeze promotion of the affected claim family and add an explicit caveat before further execution. |
| External replication cannot reproduce an internal decision record | Maximum allowed status is `internal-support` until the discrepancy is resolved. |
