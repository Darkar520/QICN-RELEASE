Claim Factorization via Boolean σ-Algebras

The v26–v28 documents assumed that a claim C : X → {0, 1} “factors through” the invariants
{F1 , . . . , Fk }, i.e., C(x) = h(F1 (x), . . . , Fk (x)). This assumption was never justified. We now
provide the measure-theoretic conditions under which such factorization holds.
Definition 7 (Claim algebra). Let A be a σ-algebra on X. A claim is a A-measurable function
C : X → {0, 1}. Equivalently, C is the indicator function of a set EC ∈ A: C = IEC .

Falsification Conditions and QICN Status

Theorem 5 is a proved mathematical statement. Its applicability to QICN depends on verifying
hypotheses H1–H4 for the specific QICN ontology.
Non-Claim 2 (H1 verification status: open). The QICN framework has not proved that its
latent state space X is compact Hausdorff, nor that its observation channel π is continuous.
The topology of X has not been specified. Without H1, Theorem 1 does not apply.

consistent with the fiber diameter being far larger than 2εi for the declared εi = 0.05. No formal
computation of ωi (y) has been performed for any QICN invariant.
Non-Claim 4 (H3 verification status: failed). No proof has been given that any QICN claim
