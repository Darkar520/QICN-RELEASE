## Reenunciation of the Target

A candidate is not a genuine `S` merely because a tuple can be written down. A
future candidate must provide:

```text
S = (X, Phi, C, R, Gamma, U)
```

with:

- `X`: a nontrivial metrizable internal state space.
- `Phi`: nondegenerate admissible transition operators on `X`.
- `C`: effective causal structure used for factorization and interventions.
- `R`: readouts that separate relevant support states.
- `Gamma`: admissibility constraints defining `X_Gamma`.
- `U`: a nontrivial intervention set, not a singleton chosen to avoid tests.
- `A`: a single admissible support satisfying all six invariants jointly.
- `Delta(S)`: explicit positive margins with `delta_star(S)>0`.
- `Cert(S)`: evidence-localized certificate for the same support.

## Anti-Vacuity Conditions by Invariant

### Iper - Structural Persistence

Canonical anchor: `07_operational_criterion_absorbed.tex:101-107`.

Genuine satisfaction requires:

- `A` is non-empty and compact.
- `Phi_u(A) subset A` for every `u in U`.
- `A` intersects or converges into a non-empty admissible attractor region.
- `A` stays at explicit distance `delta_per(S)>0` from the collapse set.
- `A` is not a single point chosen only because every map fixes it.

Trivial satisfaction is disallowed when:

- the support is a singleton with no demonstrated nontrivial neighborhood,
- `Phi` is constant on `A`,
- `U` is chosen so narrowly that invariance is never challenged,
- the collapse set is undefined or made empty by convention.

Pass condition:

```text
PASS_Iper iff A is nonempty, compact, Phi_u-invariant for all u, and
delta_per(S) >= epsilon_per > 0 is explicitly witnessed on nontrivial support.
```

### Iri - Rigid Identity

Canonical anchor: `07_operational_criterion_absorbed.tex:112-113`.

Genuine satisfaction requires:

- the readout family induced by `R` on `A` determines a unique identity object
  `Id_S`;
- there is an explicit gap `delta_ri(S)>0`;
- admissible alternative identity candidates exist or are generated by a
  documented admissible perturbation class;
- the uniqueness gap is measured against those alternatives, not against an
  empty candidate set.

Trivial satisfaction is disallowed when:

- identity is unique only because no alternatives were defined,
- `R` has only one constant output,
- candidate alternatives are excluded by definition rather than by distance,
- the identity object is simply named into existence.

Pass condition:

```text
PASS_Iri iff Id_S is unique against a nonempty admissible alternative family
and every alternative remains at deformation distance >= delta_ri(S)>0.
```

### Iint - Causal Integration

Canonical anchor: `07_operational_criterion_absorbed.tex:118-123`.

Genuine satisfaction requires:

- no nontrivial factorization `A=A1 x A2` and `R=R1 disjoint_union R2` can
  reproduce admissible histories below the positive margin `delta_int(S)`;
- decomposed dynamics and causal structure are tested, not ignored;
- the candidate is not a product system re-labeled as atomic;
- atomicity is established by structural truth, not by absence of listed
  partitions.

Operational connection to Phase 7:

- `docs/ai-platform-outputs/sims/phase7/qicn_phase7_atomicity_ground_truth.js`
  computes atomicity from `n` and `transition_table` only.
- Lines 82-110 define `computeAtomicityTruth`, returning
  `FACTORIZABLE_NON_ATOMIC` or `NON_FACTORIZABLE_ATOMIC` with truth source
  `BRUTE_FORCE_DYNAMIC_FACTORIZATION_FROM_N_AND_TRANSITION_TABLE_ONLY`.
- Lines 124-145 audit that labels such as `family` and `edges` are forbidden
  inputs to the truth source.

For a finite/discrete candidate, a genuine `Iint` witness must pass an analogous
brute-force or exhaustive structural factorization check. For a continuous
candidate, the document must define the admissible factorization class and show
why the nonfactorization claim is not a consequence of leaving factorization
candidates unspecified.

Trivial satisfaction is disallowed when:

- no partition class is defined,
- the system is dynamically a product but has coupled labels,
- `R` is chosen to hide separable components,
- `delta_int` is an arbitrary epsilon without a computable or analytic lower
  bound.

Pass condition:

```text
PASS_Iint iff every admissible nontrivial product/factor model fails to
reproduce histories and identity below delta_int(S)>0, with the factorization
search defined independently of construction labels.
```

### Icont - Regime Continuity

Canonical anchor: `07_operational_criterion_absorbed.tex:128-137`.

Genuine satisfaction requires:

- a complete metric space `(E,d_E)`;
- a concrete assignment `Pi_S:A -> E`;
- continuity of induced regime paths over admissible windows;
- an explicit `delta_cont(S)>0` bounding fragmentation on all admissible
  histories;
- perturbation tests showing no finite-energy admissible perturbation jumps to
  an incompatible regime while preserving `Id_S`.

Trivial satisfaction is disallowed when:

- `Pi_S` is constant by construction;
- `E` has the indiscrete or collapsed metric;
- only a single trajectory is tested;
- discontinuities are hidden by redefining the regime space.

Pass condition:

```text
PASS_Icont iff Pi_S maps A into a complete metric regime space, admissible
trajectories are continuous in that space, and fragmentation is bounded below
by an explicit positive margin delta_cont(S).
```

### Idiff - Non-Null Differentiation

Canonical anchor: `07_operational_criterion_absorbed.tex:143-148`.

Genuine satisfaction requires:

- distinct `x,y in A`;
- a finite horizon `T`;
- a nondegenerate readout subfamily `Read* subset R`;
- an admissible schedule `u_bullet`;
- separated readout histories by `delta_diff(S)>0`;
- the null class is not an asymptotically stable admissible attractor.

Trivial satisfaction is disallowed when:

- `x=y`, or the apparent separation comes from labels outside state/readout
  dynamics;
- readouts are constant or duplicate each other;
- histories differ only by uncalibrated noise;
- the null class is removed by fiat rather than tested dynamically.

Pass condition:

```text
PASS_Idiff iff there exist x,y in A whose admissible readout histories are
separated by >= delta_diff(S)>0 under a specified schedule and nondegenerate
readout family.
```

### Ileg - Operational Legibility

Canonical anchors: `07_operational_criterion_absorbed.tex:153-168` and
`07_operational_criterion_absorbed.tex:277-290`.

Genuine satisfaction requires:

- certified readout subfamily `Read_leg subset R`;
- decoder family `Dec_S` over windows of length at least `tau_min(S)`;
- class set `Class_S`;
- legibility margin `delta_leg(S)>0`;
- separability, noise robustness, persistence window, intervention fidelity,
  negative controls, and structured compressibility jointly.

Trivial satisfaction is disallowed when:

- the decoder memorizes construction labels or state IDs;
- the class set is opaque and not recoverable from readouts;
- negative controls are absent;
- sham/off-target interventions produce the same class signature;
- compression collapses the partition.

Pass condition:

```text
PASS_Ileg iff a certified decoder recovers the partition from admissible
readouts under noise, controls, persistence windows, negative controls, and
compression constraints with delta_leg(S)>0.
```

## Global Anti-Toy Guards

A candidate fails immediately if any guard below fails:

1. `delta_star(S)>0` must have an explicit lower bound. No informal "take
   epsilon small enough" margins.
2. `X` must be nontrivial: at least two distinguishable states on or near the
   support, or a documented nontrivial continuum.
3. `Phi` must be neither globally constant nor the identity-only dynamics on
   the relevant support.
4. `R` must separate points or histories on the support; all-constant readouts
   fail.
5. `U` must be nontrivial; a singleton intervention set is allowed only if a
   separate argument shows it does not remove necessary tests.
6. `A` must be common across all six invariants; no invariant-specific support
   switching.
7. No invariant may be satisfied by a universal quantifier over an empty class.
8. No invariant may be satisfied by construction labels that the detector or
   decoder reads directly.
9. Factorization, identity alternatives, perturbations, controls, and negative
   controls must be specified before scoring.
10. The certificate must include a traceable dependency ledger linking every
    invariant witness to its evidence and margin.

## Internal vs External Boundary

Passing this checklist would certify only internal model conformity to a
non-canonical review bar. It would not imply:

- external validation;
- membership of any real-world system in `Crit_op`;
- consciousness, identity, subjectivity, agency, phenomenality, CCR,
  `I_int`, or no-vacuity claims;
- bridge completion between small computational models and the public theory.

At most, a passing candidate becomes eligible for human review as a concrete
internal model instance.

## Candidate Checklist

| Gate | Pass/fail question | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| System tuple | Is `S=(X,Phi,C,R,Gamma,U)` fully specified? | Definitions of all six components and domains/codomains. |
| Support | Is there one common admissible `A`? | Nonempty compact invariant support intersecting attractor and avoiding collapse. |
| Margins | Is `delta_star(S)>0` explicit? | Numeric/computable/analytic lower bound for each margin. |
| Iper | Is persistence nontrivial? | Forward invariance plus non-collapse margin on nontrivial support. |
| Iri | Is identity unique against real alternatives? | Alternative family and positive deformation gap. |
| Iint | Is nonfactorization structurally true? | Exhaustive or analytically justified factorization test independent of labels. |
| Icont | Is regime assignment continuous into a complete metric space? | `Pi_S`, metric `E`, continuity proof/test, positive fragmentation margin. |
| Idiff | Are histories genuinely separated? | Distinct states, schedule, readout subfamily, horizon, positive separation. |
| Ileg | Is the partition recoverable under controls? | Decoder, controls, negative controls, noise robustness, compression audit. |
| Non-circularity | Are detectors/decoders blind to construction labels? | Input-contract audit and ablations. |
| Internal boundary | Is the claim limited to internal conformity? | Explicit non-external-validation statement. |

Decision rule:

```text
GENUINE_INTERNAL_S_CANDIDATE iff all checklist rows PASS and no toy pattern
below is present.
```

## Prohibited Toy Patterns

The following patterns disqualify a candidate unless explicitly neutralized by
additional evidence:

1. Product system relabeled as integrated to fake `Iint`.
2. Dynamics globally constant on `A`.
3. Identity-only dynamics with no perturbation/intervention challenge.
4. Singleton support used to satisfy persistence, continuity, and legibility
   automatically.
5. Empty admissible alternative set used to fake `Iri`.
6. Empty factorization class used to fake `Iint`.
7. Constant regime map `Pi_S` used to fake `Icont`.
8. Identical or all-zero readouts used to fake legibility.
9. Decoder memorizes labels, construction family, state IDs, or partition
   names instead of recovering structure from readouts.
10. Margins declared as arbitrary epsilon without lower-bound derivation.
11. `U` singleton chosen to avoid rupture, negative controls, or intervention
    fidelity tests.
12. Collapse set excluded by definition rather than distance or dynamics.
13. Readout separation caused by uncalibrated noise rather than state/history
    structure.
14. Class partition opaque to audit or not recoverable under compression.
15. Switching supports across invariants so that no single `A` carries all six.
