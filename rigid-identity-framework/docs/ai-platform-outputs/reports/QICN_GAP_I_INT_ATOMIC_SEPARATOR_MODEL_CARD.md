## Definitions Extracted

### `I_int`

Paper 5 defines causal integration as the absence of a non-trivial factorization of admissible state space and readouts, with decomposed dynamics and causal structure, that reproduces admissible histories within a positive integration margin while preserving identity (`paper5_operational_consciousness/main.tex:306-318`).

The v20 scaffold defines a factorization-gap functional:

`Delta_int(S)` is the infimum over non-trivial admissible factorizations of history, identity, response, and gauge-loss terms (`I_INT_ATOMIC_SEPARATOR_CLOSURE_v20.tex:198-204`). It sets `I_int(S)=1` exactly when `Delta_int(S)>0`, with `Delta_int(S)=+infty` if there are no non-trivial admissible factorizations (`I_INT_ATOMIC_SEPARATOR_CLOSURE_v20.tex:205-208`).

### Faithful Factorization

Paper 5 defines an exact factorization `F:S -> S_1 x S_2` as intervention-faithful when it preserves every element of the operational separator family generated by histories, intervention responses, and the rigid identity object, and reflects admissible equality from factor-level histories, responses, and identity image (`paper5_operational_consciousness/main.tex:326-337`).

### Atomic Separator

Paper 5 states the atomicity condition as: no partition of `Theta_S` into two proper factor-local separator families can preserve identity, history, and intervention-response reflection simultaneously (`paper5_operational_consciousness/main.tex:339-344`).

The v20 scaffold states the same gap more explicitly: `Theta_S` is atomic when there is no exact non-trivial admissible factorization `F:S -> S_1 x S_2` admitting a factor-local separator partition that preserves `Id_S`, `Hist_S`, and `Resp_S` simultaneously (`I_INT_ATOMIC_SEPARATOR_CLOSURE_v20.tex:102-104`). It warns that "atomic separator" is a formal non-decomposition predicate and that merely restating it under another name would be circular (`I_INT_ATOMIC_SEPARATOR_CLOSURE_v20.tex:106-108`).
