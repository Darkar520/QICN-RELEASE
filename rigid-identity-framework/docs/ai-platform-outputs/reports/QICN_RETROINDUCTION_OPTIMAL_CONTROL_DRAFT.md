## 1. Recovery of the Axiom Core

[CONJECTURE] Local source search did not locate a canonical BaseCore or paper statement named `Axioma III`, `Axiom III`, `retro-induccion`, or `retro-induction`. Therefore the following is treated as a prompt-level historical/proposed axiom, not a corpus citation.

[CONJECTURE] Reconstructed Axiom III, historical form: a present action can be selected by "retro-induction temporal", meaning that projected future consequences constrain present choice.

[STANDARD_APPLICATION] Discarded part: any reading in which the future physically causes the present is rejected. That reading would be outside the BaseCore forward-only transition grammar.

[STANDARD_APPLICATION] Conserved kernel: at time `t`, the system evaluates a model-generated family of future trajectories

```text
x_t, x_{t+1}, ..., x_{t+H}
```

generated by forward transitions, and chooses the present admissible intervention `u_t` that optimizes a finite-horizon objective.

[STANDARD_APPLICATION] Neighbor literature: this is the standard shape of dynamic programming / Bellman recursion, model predictive control, and active-inference policy selection under expected free energy. Verified external anchors used for this draft:

- Bellman, R. E. `Dynamic Programming`, Princeton University Press, 1957; standard Bellman recursion reference. Related primary article anchor: https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.38.8.716
- Rawlings, Mayne, and Diehl, `Model Predictive Control: Theory, Computation, and Design`, 2nd ed., 2017; standard MPC reference. Web anchor: https://sites.engineering.ucsb.edu/~jbraw/mpc/MPC-book-2nd-edition-5th-printing.pdf

## 2. Formalization over `C_op`

### 2.1 State and admissibility layer

[STANDARD_APPLICATION] Let

```text
S = (X, Phi, C, R, Gamma, U)
```

be a Paper 5 admissible system candidate. Paper 5 defines this tuple and its components at `paper5_operational_consciousness/main.tex:176-195`, with `Phi = {Phi_u}_{u in U}` a family of transition operators and `U` the admissible intervention set.

[STANDARD_APPLICATION] Let `A_S subset X` be the admissible support. Paper 5 requires forward invariance, `Phi_u(A_S) subseteq A_S` for every admissible `u`, at `paper5_operational_consciousness/main.tex:197-204`.

[STANDARD_APPLICATION] Let `C_op` be used only as a conditional domain marker: if `S in C_op` and the certificate assumptions hold, then the lookahead problem may be posed on `A_S`. This draft does not certify that any concrete system belongs to `C_op`.

### 2.2 Forward dynamics

[STANDARD_APPLICATION] A controlled trajectory is generated by

```text
x_{k+1} = Phi_{u_k}(x_k),     u_k in U_adm(x_k),     k = 0,...,H-1.
```

This matches Paper 5's operational-history equation `x_{t+1} = Phi_{u_t}(x_t)` at `paper5_operational_consciousness/main.tex:216-220`.

[STANDARD_APPLICATION] For BaseCore-compatible versions, use the BaseCore transition

```text
T_u(Psi) = aleph(K Psi + Gamma(u)).
```

BaseCore defines `T_u` at `basecore/core/sections/01_foundation_from_core.tex:112-117`, assumes `||K|| < 1` at `01_foundation_from_core.tex:39-40`, proves strict contractivity at `01_foundation_from_core.tex:119-135`, and derives unique fixed points via Banach at `01_foundation_from_core.tex:140-152`.

### 2.3 Finite-horizon value functional

[STANDARD_APPLICATION] Fix horizon `H >= 1`, discount `beta in [0,1]`, stage cost `ell : A_S x U -> R`, and terminal cost `g : A_S -> R`. For a control sequence `u_{0:H-1}`, define

```text
J_H(x_0; u_{0:H-1})
  = sum_{k=0}^{H-1} beta^k ell(x_k, u_k) + beta^H g(x_H)

x_{k+1} = Phi_{u_k}(x_k).
```

[STANDARD_APPLICATION] The finite-horizon optimal value is

```text
V_H(x) = inf_{u_{0:H-1}} J_H(x; u_{0:H-1})
```

with the infimum taken only over admissible intervention sequences that keep the trajectory in `A_S`.

[STANDARD_APPLICATION] Bellman recursion, finite horizon:

```text
V_0(x) = g(x)
V_{h+1}(x) = inf_{u in U_adm(x)}
  [ ell(x,u) + beta V_h(Phi_u(x)) ].
```

[STANDARD_APPLICATION] A receding-horizon policy selects the first action of an optimizer:

```text
pi_H(x) in argmin_{u in U_adm(x)}
  [ ell(x,u) + beta V_{H-1}(Phi_u(x)) ].
```

If the argmin does not exist, use `epsilon`-optimal selectors and record this as an implementation limitation.

### 2.4 Interpretation

[STANDARD_APPLICATION] The recursion computes value functions backward in the planning index, but all state transitions remain forward. This is lookahead, not reverse causation.

[CONJECTURE] The only QICN-specific difference worth exploring is the choice of `ell` and `g`: they may penalize predicted loss of `C_op` certificate margins, decoder failure, invariant rupture, or departure from admissible support. That is a proposed objective design, not a theorem.

## 3. Consistency with Forward-Only Contraction

Verdict: `CONSISTENT_UNDER_RESTRICTED_ADMISSIBLE_CONTROL`.

[STANDARD_APPLICATION] Assume every admissible `T_u` used by the policy satisfies the BaseCore contraction hypotheses with a common bound `||K|| <= c < 1`, and assume the selected interventions stay inside the admissible set. Then every predicted rollout is a composition of forward maps:

```text
T_{u_{H-1}} o ... o T_{u_0}.
```

[STANDARD_APPLICATION] A composition of contractions with common Lipschitz bound `c` is contractive with bound at most `c^H`. This uses standard metric-space reasoning; it is not a new QICN theorem.

[STANDARD_APPLICATION] The Bellman recursion optimizes over candidate controls using model predictions. It does not modify the causal rule `x_{t+1}=T_u(x_t)` and does not introduce any dependence of `x_t` on `x_{t+1}`.

[STANDARD_APPLICATION] Therefore this formulation does not conflict with BaseCore's forward-only contractive dynamics when interventions are restricted to the admissible control family already covered by the contraction assumptions.

[CONJECTURE] If a proposed "retro-inductive" policy is allowed to choose interventions outside the contraction/admissibility envelope, then consistency fails as a QICN layer. That failure would be a boundary violation, not a discovery.
