## Hypothesis And Rival

- Observable: Certified class label and Qop geometry across implementations.
- Manipulation: Compare a cross-substrate pair with preserved invariant margins.
- Framework prediction: The pair remains in the same operational class with epsilon-bounded Qop geometry under the admissible equivalence regime.
- Rival prediction: A substrate-privilege rival predicts class divergence based on substrate label despite preserved structure.
- Support condition: The two implementations preserve all registered invariant margins and remain class-equivalent under frozen geometry tolerance.
- Weakening condition: Equivalence holds only after changing tolerance, using non-matched implementations, or ignoring an unresolved identity-margin ambiguity.
- Destruction condition: Class divergence or Qop geometry mismatch appears despite verified preserved structure.
