## Hypothesis And Rival

- Observable: Class membership and Qop geometry before and after targeted rupture.
- Manipulation: Perform a one-invariant ablation while preserving comparable substrate and gross scale.
- Framework prediction: Verified loss of a critical invariant causes certified class exit, contraction, or undefined Qop when the pre-ablation invariant headroom exceeds the registered ambiguity margin delta_amb. If the pre-ablation headroom is at or below delta_amb, the result is classified as boundary-ambiguous weakening rather than support.
- Rival prediction: A complexity-only or gross-scale rival predicts little or no class-relevant change when complexity and scale are preserved.
- Support condition: The targeted invariant loss is verified, the pre-ablation headroom exceeds the registered ambiguity margin delta_amb, sham/off-target controls do not produce the same pattern, and the class exits, contracts, or becomes undefined according to frozen rules.
- Weakening condition: The result falls inside the pre-declared boundary-ambiguous margin, changes only under global degradation, or depends on nonselective damage that also affects matched controls.
- Destruction condition: The system remains certified without downgrade after verified invariant loss outside the registered ambiguity margin.
