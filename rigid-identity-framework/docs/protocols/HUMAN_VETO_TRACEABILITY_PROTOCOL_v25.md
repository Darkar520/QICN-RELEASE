# Human Veto Traceability Protocol v25

Governance boundary: this protocol does not certify external support, consciousness, phenomenality, identity transfer, bridge-burden closure, or human mathematical review. It defines the minimum traceability needed before a human decision may affect registry status.

## Required fields for a veto or approval

A human review decision record must include:

- reviewer role and independence statement;
- conflict-of-interest declaration;
- artifact hashes reviewed;
- exact claim IDs affected;
- decision: `approve_downgrade`, `veto_downgrade`, `request_more_evidence`, or `escalate_to_external_panel`;
- rationale tied to formal criteria, not author preference;
- timestamp and signature or equivalent externally verifiable attestation.

## Prohibited actions

- No LLM-generated review may be recorded as human-curated.
- No unsigned email, chat statement, or self-review may upgrade a claim.
- A veto cannot convert a failed adjudication into support. It can only block an automatic registry edit pending review.

## Registry effect

Until a compliant record exists, all downgrade outputs remain `dry_run_only`. The registry is not modified automatically.
