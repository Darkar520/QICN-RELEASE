# Atomic Separator Lemma Attempt

Status: OPEN_BURDEN_REFINED / NO_CLAIM_UPGRADE  
Date: 2026-05-26

## Boundary

This document attempts to localize the atomic-separator burden for `I_int`. It
does not prove the unconditional integration-transfer proposition, does not
validate consciousness or phenomenality, and does not provide external evidence.

## A. Definitions

### System

A system is an admissible operational object `S` with admissible support
`A_S`, histories, readouts, interventions, and a rigid identity object where
those terms are already defined in the corpus.

### Admissible factorization

An admissible factorization is an exact map `F:S -> S_1 x S_2` whose projections
preserve the declared admissible structure required by the relevant criterion.
This is a mathematical/model-layer notion, not a physical decomposition claim.

### Intervention-faithful factorization

A factorization is intervention-faithful when it preserves the operational
separator family generated by histories, intervention responses, and the rigid
identity object, and reflects admissible equality in the quotient.

### Atomic operational separator

A separator family `Theta_S` is atomic when it cannot be partitioned into two
proper factor-local separator families that jointly preserve identity, history,
and intervention-response reflection.

### Rigid identity

Rigid identity means the framework-internal inverse/projective identity object
is unique under the stated compatibility and non-collapse assumptions.

### Continuity

Continuity means admissible histories vary in the declared operational topology
without fragmentation beyond the stated tolerance or hypothesis.

### Intervention fidelity

Intervention fidelity means admissible interventions preserve the intended
readout/response relation and are not explained away by sham, off-target,
bookkeeping, or decoder artifacts.

### Integration transfer

Integration transfer is the proposed implication from rigid identity,
continuity, and intervention fidelity to non-factorization/integration. In the
current registry this remains an open burden unless atomicity is derived.

### Triviality of factorization

A factorization is trivial up to admissible isomorphism when one factor carries
the full separator and the other is null/bookkeeping-only in the admissible
quotient.

## B. Minimal assumptions tested

```text
A1: rigid identity object exists and is unique.
A2: admissible histories are continuous in the declared operational topology.
A3: interventions are faithful to the declared response channels.
A4: factorization preserves admissible histories and intervention responses.
```

The target claim would require:

```text
A1 + A2 + A3 + A4 => atomic operational separator Theta_S.
```

## C. Proof attempt

1. From A1, the identity object is unique.  
   Type: theorem/imported assumption.

2. From A2, histories cannot fragment outside the declared continuity regime.  
   Type: theorem/imported assumption.

3. From A3, interventions cannot be replaced by sham/off-target response
   bookkeeping without loss.  
   Type: theorem/imported assumption.

4. To derive atomicity, one must show that any proper partition
   `Theta_S = Theta_1 union Theta_2` fails to reflect admissible equality or
   loses identity/history/response information.  
   Type: required lemma.

5. Existing assumptions do not by themselves exclude weakly coupled products
   where a global identity label is unique, histories are continuous on the
   product, and interventions are faithful componentwise, while the separator
   decomposes as `Theta_1 x Theta_2`.  
   Type: gap / counterexample candidate.

## D. Counterexample candidate

Candidate system:

```text
S = S_1 x S_2
Theta_S = Theta_1 union Theta_2
identity object = ordered pair of component identities
history topology = product topology
interventions = componentwise faithful interventions
```

Observed status:

- rigid identity may hold for the ordered-pair identity object;
- continuity may hold in the product topology;
- intervention fidelity may hold componentwise;
- atomicity can fail because the separator decomposes into proper factor-local
  separator families.

This is not a fully formal counterexample to the whole corpus because the exact
admissibility category may add extra non-product constraints. It is enough to
block the unconditional proof attempt from the currently listed assumptions.

## E. Result

Result: OPEN_BURDEN_REFINED.

The conditional theorem already in Paper 5 remains valid under the explicit
atomic-separator hypothesis. The attempted upstream implication

```text
rigid identity + continuity + intervention fidelity
    => atomic operational separator Theta_S
```

is not proved from the current assumptions. A plausible product-separator
counterexample family shows why an additional non-product, observability, or
separator-completeness assumption is required.

## Additional assumptions that may close the route

A future proof may become possible under one or more explicit assumptions:

- finite atomic intervention basis;
- separability/completeness of the intervention algebra;
- faithful decoder with complete separator observability;
- nondegenerate cross-component intervention response;
- prohibition of product-decomposable identity/history/response separators.

No such assumption is silently added here.
