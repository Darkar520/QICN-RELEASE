\section*{Draft Lemma: Factorization Triviality for \(I_{\mathrm{int}}\)}

\paragraph{Boundary.}
This draft is a proof attempt and burden localization artifact. It is not a
closed theorem of the corpus and must not be used to upgrade
\texttt{prop:integration-transfer}.

\begin{lemma}[Factorization triviality under strong admissibility]
Let
\[
S=(X,\Phi,U,H,\mathrm{Id},R)
\]
be an admissible causal-information system. Let
\[
F:S\to S_1\times S_2
\]
be an admissible factorization preserving operational histories, intervention
responses, and the rigid identity object. If the preservation of the identity
object is faithful in the sense that any morphism preserving
\(\mathrm{Id}\), \(H\), and \(R\) reflects admissible equality of histories,
then \(F\) is trivial up to admissible isomorphism.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Proof attempt]
Let \(\pi_i:S_1\times S_2\to S_i\) be the product projections. Since \(F\) is
admissible, each composite \(\pi_i\circ F\) preserves the declared history and
response structure on its image. By faithful preservation of the rigid identity
object, any two states of \(S\) that are separated by an admissible history or
intervention response must remain separated under the identity-preserving image.
Therefore any projection that forgets information relevant to
\(\mathrm{Id}\), \(H\), or \(R\) is not admissible.

Assume \(F\) is non-trivial. Then neither projection is an admissible
isomorphism to \(S\), and at least one projection discards or externalizes a
history, response, or identity-channel dependency. But this contradicts the
faithful preservation condition. Hence the only admissible factorizations are
those in which one factor is isomorphic to \(S\) and the other factor is null
or bookkeeping-only. This is the trivial factorization class.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Unclosed step.}
The proof depends on the strong faithfulness condition. The current Paper 5
prose does not yet derive that condition from upstream rigidity, continuity,
and forensic intervention fidelity. Without it, weakly coupled product systems
may preserve aggregate histories while distributing causal dependencies across
factors.

\paragraph{Required next lemma.}
Prove that upstream rigid identity, phenomenological continuity, and
intervention fidelity imply faithful preservation of the identity/history/
response triple. If this cannot be proved, \(I_{\mathrm{int}}\) must remain an
open burden or be replaced by a directly measured integration estimator.
