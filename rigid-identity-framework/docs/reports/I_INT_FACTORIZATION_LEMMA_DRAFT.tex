\section*{Draft Lemma: Factorization Triviality for \(I_{\mathrm{int}}\)}

\paragraph{Boundary.}
This draft records the v17 proof upgrade. It proves a conditional
factorization-triviality theorem under an explicit atomic separator hypothesis.
It must not be read as an unconditional proof of
\texttt{prop:integration-transfer} until the atomic separator hypothesis is
derived from upstream rigidity, continuity, and intervention fidelity.

\begin{lemma}[Factorization triviality under an atomic operational separator]
Let
\[
S=(X,\Phi,U,H,\mathrm{Id},R)
\]
be an admissible causal-information system. Let
\[
F:S\to S_1\times S_2
\]
be an exact factorization preserving operational histories, intervention
responses, and the rigid identity object. Let \(\Theta_S\) be the operational
separator family generated by those histories, responses, and identity maps.
If \(\Theta_S\) is atomic, in the sense that no two proper factor-local
subfamilies jointly reflect admissible equality while preserving identity,
history, and intervention responses, then \(F\) is trivial up to admissible
isomorphism.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Let \(\pi_i:S_1\times S_2\to S_i\) be the product projections. If both factors
were proper, the histories, intervention responses, and identity information
visible through \(\pi_1\circ F\) and \(\pi_2\circ F\) would define two proper
factor-local separator subfamilies of \(\Theta_S\). Since \(F\) is exact, the
product of those factor-local readings would still preserve the operational
history, response, and identity readings of \(S\). Since \(F\) is assumed to
reflect admissible equality, those two proper subfamilies would jointly reflect
admissible equality on \(S\). This contradicts atomicity of \(\Theta_S\).
Therefore at least one projection carries the full separator family and is an
admissible isomorphism onto \(S\) in the operational quotient. The remaining
factor is null or bookkeeping-only. Thus \(F\) is trivial up to admissible
isomorphism.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Closed conditional step.}
The conditional lemma is now closed: atomicity of the operational separator
implies factorization triviality. This is stronger than the v16 proof attempt,
because the missing condition is no longer implicit; it is named, typed, and
used exactly once.

\paragraph{Required next lemma.}
Prove that upstream rigid identity, phenomenological continuity, and forensic
intervention fidelity imply atomicity of \(\Theta_S\). If this cannot be
proved, Proposition~\texttt{prop:integration-transfer} remains open even though
the conditional factorization theorem is proved.
