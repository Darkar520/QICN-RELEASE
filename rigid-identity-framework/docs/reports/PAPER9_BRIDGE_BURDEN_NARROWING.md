# Paper 9 Bridge Burden Narrowing v1

Status: FCR v13 bridge-risk reduction scaffold.

Date: 2026-05-25

## Boundary

This document narrows one Paper 9 bridge predicate into a concrete burden
bundle. It does not execute BPF-2, BPF-3, BPF-4, BPF-5, or BPF-6. It does not
claim bridge admissibility, phenomenality, human equivalence, external
validation, moral status, or metaphysical subjecthood.

## Reason For Narrowing

Paper 9 deliberately introduces a broad bridge predicate family:

```text
Pi_D, Pi_V, Pi_W, Pi_A, Pi_E, Pi_T
```

That architecture is disciplined, but it creates a wide test surface. The next
scientific move should not add new predicates. It should bind one predicate
group to:

- an observable family;
- a matched intervention;
- a named rival;
- a penalized loss;
- a destruction condition.

## Selected First Predicate Group

Selected group: `Pi_D`, phenomenal differentiation predicate.

Rationale:

- It is the least dependent on embodied closure.
- It can be attacked by strong rivals such as semantic density, report
  richness, and narrative coherence.
- It can reuse Paper 8 self-index, ownership, and irreducibility specs without
  pretending those specs already prove bridge admissibility.

## Observable Family

Bridge differentiation should be measured as a selective residual after
subtracting upstream Paper 8 proxies:

```text
BridgeDiffResidual =
  Loss(best semantic/report rival on bridge tasks)
  - Loss(Pi_D candidate model on the same tasks)
```

This residual is admissible only if the rival is frozen before execution and
the candidate model cannot directly copy the upstream Paper 8 coordinate.

## Intervention Family

Minimum BPF-2 design for `Pi_D`:

1. baseline trace collection;
2. semantic-density preserving perturbation;
3. report-richness preserving perturbation;
4. self-index/ownership targeted perturbation;
5. sham perturbation with identical logging path.

The intervention succeeds only if the target perturbation produces a selective
delta not reproduced by semantic-density or report-richness preservation.

## Rival Family

Primary rivals:

- `RIVAL-SEMANTIC-01`
- `RIVAL-NARRATIVE-01`
- `RIVAL-TRACE-MEMORY-01` when the task is finite-trace based

The rival wins if it reaches equal or lower penalized loss on the same bridge
observable bundle.

## Destruction Condition

`Pi_D` cannot support bridge irreducibility if any frozen rival or admissible
hybrid reaches equal or lower penalized loss while preserving the same
observable performance.

## Weakening Condition

`Pi_D` remains formal-program only if:

- thresholds are symbolic;
- interventions are not executed;
- rival families are not frozen;
- the bridge estimate is a monotone transform of an upstream Paper 8 coordinate;
- artifacts disagree about provenance or status.

## Support Condition

The strongest support available before external adjudication is internal-only:

- BPF-2 targeted interventions executed;
- BPF-3 rivals executed;
- BPF-4 thresholds frozen;
- bridge estimate not reducible to upstream proxy;
- decision record emitted;
- non-claim boundary preserved.

Even then, the result is bridge-program support, not phenomenality simpliciter.

## Implementation Decision

For v13, Paper 9 should not be broadened. The next implementation wave should
turn this document into a BPF-2/BPF-3 task bundle for `Pi_D` only.
