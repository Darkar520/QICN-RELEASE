## Phase 2 technical result

Phase 2 did not produce a global impossibility theorem for all possible QICN formalisms. That is
important: a missing instantiation is not the same as a contradiction. The honest result is narrower
and stronger for publication hygiene:

1. H1 remains under-specified rather than impossible.
2. H2 is blocked because operational constants are not derived Lipschitz constants on `(X,d_X)`.
3. H3 is not derivable from merely naming six invariants; hidden claim-relevant coordinates remain possible.
4. H4 fails on the current operational margin and cannot be closed by post-hoc threshold edits.

## Non-derivability content

The Phase 2 ledger supplies a countermodel for H3-style overclaiming: extend the invariant coordinate
space with an independent binary coordinate `b`, let the six `F_i` ignore `b`, and let claim `C=b`.
Then two states can agree on all six invariants while disagreeing on the claim. This does not prove
future H3 impossible; it proves that H3 requires an additional level-set constancy theorem or an
axiom excluding hidden claim-relevant coordinates.

## Required future work

Before any bridge closure can be claimed, QICN must provide:

- a concrete external `X`, topology, observation map `\pi`, and admissible set `A`;
- derived Lipschitz constants `K_i` and universal fiber/oscillation bounds;
- a formal claim `C` and a proof that `C\in\sigma(F_1,\ldots,F_k)`;
- preregistered data, frozen tolerances, and a positive robustness margin for H4.
